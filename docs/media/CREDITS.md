photographs and aerial tiles they are drawn on belong to their authors, under the terms below.
Boxes, labels and captions were added; nothing else was changed.

## `obb-detections.gif`

Six 1024×1024 tiles from Ultralytics' [dota8](https://docs.ultralytics.com/datasets/obb/dota8/)
sample, which Ultralytics distributes under GPL-3.0. The imagery comes from
[DOTA-v1.0](https://captain-whu.github.io/DOTA/) (Xia et al., CVPR 2018), whose authors make it
available for academic use; the underlying sources include Google Earth.
